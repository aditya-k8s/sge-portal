How a job runs

From drawing to despatch

Every order moves through the same route, and you can see which stage it has reached at any time from the client portal.

  1. 01

    MATERIAL

    Stock received, checked against the order and logged.

  2. 02

    CUTTING

    Sawing and rough turning to the billet size the part needs.

  3. 03

    MACHINING

    CNC and VMC operations: milling, boring, drilling, tapping.

  4. 04

    FINISHING

    Heat treatment, grinding and surface finish to drawing.

  5. 05

    INSPECTION

    Dimensional check against the drawing, then despatch.

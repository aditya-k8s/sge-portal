About Us

Who We Are

A precision machining company built on expertise, investment in technology, and commitment to quality.

Our Story

Shri Gouri Engineers was founded with a clear vision: to provide manufacturers and OEMs with reliable, high-precision machined components at competitive prices. From a humble beginning with a single lathe machine, we have grown into a fully equipped precision machining facility.

Today, our facility houses CNC machining centres, VMC machines, BMC machines, and conventional lathes. Each machine is operated by skilled machinists trained to deliver parts to your exact drawings and specifications.

We serve clients across the automotive, industrial, and manufacturing sectors — handling both prototype runs and high-volume production orders.

Our Mission

To deliver precision-machined components that meet the highest quality standards, on time, every time — supporting our clients' production schedules and reputation.

Quality Commitment

Every component undergoes dimensional inspection before dispatch. We maintain inspection records and offer traceability on all jobs. Our quality culture starts on the shop floor, not the inspection room.

Infrastructure

Modern CNC, VMC, and BMC machining centres. In-house CMM inspection. Skilled workforce. All under one roof in our dedicated manufacturing facility in Maharashtra.

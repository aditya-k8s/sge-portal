Our story

From one lathe to a machining facility

Shri Gouri Engineers was founded to give manufacturers and OEMs a dependable source of high-precision machined components at a fair price. It began with a single lathe. It is now a fully equipped precision machining facility.

The floor today runs CNC machining centres, VMC and BMC machines and conventional lathes, each operated by machinists trained to work to your drawings rather than to an approximation of them.

We supply the automotive, industrial and general manufacturing sectors, handling prototype runs and repeat production orders with the same process and the same inspection discipline.
